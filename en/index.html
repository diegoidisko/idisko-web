How it works

Join, listen and chat

Disko Room

People are online right now

Open the Disko Room and see who's there and what they're playing. You can filter by age and profession. With Premium, by country and gender too.

Messages

Music gives you the opening line

No need to know what to say: send a song with a short message. If they reply, the chat has already started.

Shuffle

If it clicks, it clicks.

Turn on Shuffle and we introduce you to someone who turned it on too. If it doesn't click, you skip. No strings attached.

VIP room

You and me, the same song

Invite someone to a private room. If they accept, you both hear the same thing at the same time: when one changes the song, it changes for both. The camera is optional.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Is iDisko free?

Yes. The essentials are free. Premium, with a 7-day free trial, adds unlimited VIP invitations (you get 10 per day for free), the country and gender filters, and shows you who opened your profile. YouTube ads are shown by YouTube's player and iDisko does not remove them.

Do I have to show my camera?

No. The camera is optional and only exists in VIP rooms and Shuffle. To turn it on you verify your age once.

Is it a dating app?

Not exactly. On iDisko you meet people through the music they're listening to. Some make friends, some find a partner, and some just want company while they cook.

What music can I listen to?

Music from YouTube and from Jamendo, which is royalty-free music. You can search for a song or pick a ready-made playlist.

Is it private?

Rooms are not recorded. The camera is moderated with automatic snapshots: if the system detects nudity, the camera is turned off and we keep one snapshot to review the case. If you think it was a mistake, write to contact@idisko.com and a person will review it.

Where can I use iDisko?

On iPhone, on Android and on the web, with the same account.

How do I delete my account?

From the app, in your account settings, or by following the steps on the Delete account page.
